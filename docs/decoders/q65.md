# Q65 / JT65

The slow weak-signal modes of WSJT-X, decoded by **jt9** of WSJT-X 2.7.0 (`lib\jt9\jt9.exe`, built by
`installer/build_jt9.sh`, its licences in `installer/JT9.txt`; `q65.py`, `static/q65.js`).

- **JT65** (60 s periods, submodes A / B / C): the older weak-signal mode of HF and EME.
- **Q65** (15, 30, 60, 120 or 300 s periods, submodes A–E = tone spacing ×1 … ×16): its successor for ionospheric and
  tropo scatter, rain scatter and EME on 6 m and above.

Both carry the messages of FT8 (calls, locator, report) and are decoded only at the end of each period.

## DECODERS › Q65/JT65

- **ON**, **MODE** (Q65 / JT65), **PERIOD** (Q65 only) and **SUB** (the submode the stations use).
- **Band keys** with the usual dial frequencies and modes: JT65 A on 160 m – 4 m (1.838 … 70.102 MHz), JT65 B on 2 m and
  70 cm, JT65 C on 23 cm, Q65 on 6 m (50.211 MHz 60 s, 50.275 MHz 30 s), 2 m (144.116 MHz), 70 cm and 23 cm; USB.
- **Progress bar** of the period being recorded.
- **Clock chip and SYNC:** the offset of the PC clock against NTP; Q65 and JT65 need it within ±1 s. SYNC sets the clock
  of Windows (with the administrator consent of Windows when needed).
- **LOG** writes every decode to `data\logs\q65\YYYY\MM\DD\q65_YYYY-MM-DD.txt` (UTC, shared by all VFOs); **CLR**
  clears the table.
- The table (newest on top): UTC, SNR (dB in 2.5 kHz), DT, DF (Hz above the dial), mode (*q1…* = decoded with a priori
  information), MHz and the message; a thin line between periods.

## How it works

The USB passband above the dial is recorded as 12 kHz audio for the whole T/R period from its UTC start, written as a
WAV file into `data\q65` and decoded there by jt9. A period during which the VFO was retuned is skipped. Tested on
simulated WAV files so far (`tests/test_q65.py`).
