RADIMCOM SDR SETUP

Connect all your SDR dongles, drag each onto its VFO, then set its ppm, upconverter and name. The receiver is stopped meanwhile, so the dongles are free.

SERIAL NUMBERS

These RTL-SDR dongles have the same (or an empty) serial number, so RADIMCOM cannot tell them apart: after replugging they could swap VFOs. A unique number can be written into their EEPROM (a backup of each goes to data\eeprom_backup). It must keep the same length.

Which dongle is which? The serial number is not printed on the dongle. Click IDENTIFY on a VFO and unplug the dongle you want there: it is found and placed by itself; then plug it back in. Share lets one dongle serve more VFOs (they tune inside the band it receives; gain, ppm and sample rate are shared). PPM = frequency correction of the dongle (0 if unknown). Upconverter: e.g. Ham It Up, LO 100 or 100.0021 MHz.
The previous VFO settings are moved to data\config_backup.
